# Word choice and lint matches

The filename remains for existing links. This is not a list of forbidden words.

Choose a word for its meaning in the sentence. Replace a vague promotional term
with a supported detail when the reader needs that detail. Changing "robust" to
"reliable" leaves an unsupported reliability claim unsupported.

Technical terms, names, quotations, and ordinary uses of words that appear in
the linter can be correct. A regex match cannot establish AI authorship, filler,
or the intended sense of a word. Inspect the surrounding passage.

Look beyond words: staged self-corrections, repeated honesty claims, forced slang,
and canned endings can remain after every flagged word is removed. Fix what the
passage says and how it connects, rather than optimizing its lint score.

Preserve identifiers, quoted source text, uncertainty, and established terminology.
Do not cycle synonyms merely to avoid repetition.
